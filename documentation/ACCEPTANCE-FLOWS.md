# English Reference Platform: baseline and acceptance flows

- Home: open `index.html` directly; use the logo, pronunciation and grammar cards, recent topics, hint chips, and header search (including `/`).
- Pronunciation: open the first lesson and notebook iframe; use its sound chips, section tabs, local search and external tab link. Open a spelling lesson, its jump links and answers, and move previous/next along the 15-lesson path.
- Grammar: open categories and topics, use section search, the topic contents, previous/next, sources and related topics.
- Tools: open word analyzer (known/unknown words and ending suggestions), grammar and tense comparisons, practice (all question types and feedback), and guided decoding (steps, level, word navigation, pronunciation).
- My Shelf: bookmark, mark as reviewed, add to the review queue from a topic; verify history, settings, and data controls. Open an invalid route.
- Bilingual acceptance: every route in both directions, no stray untranslated interface/content, identical lesson and example inventories, isolated IPA, language on reopening, switch without changing route, active search and navigation usable after switching.
- Audio acceptance: actual local decoding/playback, independent word/phrase/comparison controls, replay, stop before next playback, volume, unavailable voice message, and network-free local use. Inspect narrow and desktop layouts.
